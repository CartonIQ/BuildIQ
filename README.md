**BuildIQ** is a premium, 100% offline construction and material cost calculator designed for US homeowners, DIY enthusiasts, and contractors. 

Never guess how much paint, flooring, or concrete you need again. BuildIQ provides precise calculations, custom material pricing, and professional PDF quote generation.

---

## ✨ Features

*   🧮 **18+ Advanced Calculators:** Paint, Primer, Drywall, Flooring, Tiles, Concrete, Fencing, Landscaping, and more.
*   📄 **PDF Quote Generator:** Create, save, and share professional quotes with your company branding (Name, License, Phone, Email).
*   💰 **Custom Material Prices:** Save your local store prices, and BuildIQ will remember them for future calculations.
*   📂 **Project Management:** Save multiple projects and combine calculations into a single quote.
*   🎨 **Premium UI/UX:** Beautiful "Warm Wooden" theme with a floating glass dock, smooth animations, and Material 3 design.
*   🌙 **Dark Mode:** Full support for system dark mode.
*   🔒 **100% Offline & Private:** Your data never leaves your device.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Language:** Kotlin
*   **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
*   **UI:** XML Layouts, Material 3, ViewBinding
*   **Local Database:** Room DB (with KSP)
*   **Navigation:** Jetpack Navigation Component
*   **Monetization:** Google AdMob & UMP Consent
*   **PDF Generation:** Android Native `PdfDocument` API
